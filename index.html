Just Doors Dashboard

An internal business-operations and financial dashboard used by Just Doors Inc. (residential door replacement, Atlanta GA). It connects read-only to the company's own QuickBooks Online account to display financial reports alongside job data.

Access

This is a private, single-company application. Sign-in is restricted to Just Doors staff Google Workspace accounts (@justdoors.io) and the app runs on company infrastructure — there is no public sign-up and no third-party data access.
