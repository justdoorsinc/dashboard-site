QuickBooks has been disconnected

The connection between QuickBooks Online and the Just Doors Dashboard has been removed.

The dashboard no longer accesses your QuickBooks data. Stored access and refresh tokens are revoked with Intuit and deleted from the dashboard server. The app keeps no database of QuickBooks records — cached report data expires from memory within the hour.

To reconnect, a dashboard administrator signs in and uses the Reconnect QuickBooks link on any page banner.
